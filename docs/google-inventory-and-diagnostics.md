# Google inventory and diagnostics

## Purpose

This planned product area is intended to make diagnostic information and a
Google-inventory helper easier to orient without exposing private operational
data.

## Planned boundary and interfaces

The planned boundary covers diagnostic surfaces and a Google-inventory helper.
Its principal documented interfaces are troubleshooting, the control plane,
and the authentication-copy boundary.

- [Troubleshooting](operations/troubleshooting.md)
- [Control plane](control-plane.md)

## Geplant

Diagnostic surfaces and the Google-inventory helper are planned. This page
does not claim OAuth, credentials, an external connection, provider access,
runtime, installation, service, or live behavior.
